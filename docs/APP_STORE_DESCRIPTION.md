## Promotional text

## Description

Throttle is for people who don't want to block apps — they want to *want* them less.

Pick the apps that pull at you: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, whatever. While one of them is in the foreground, Throttle engages an on-device bandwidth limiter so the app gets painfully slow. Videos buffer. Feeds load with a lag. The hit you came for never arrives.

Two or three frustrated swipes and you put the phone down. That's the entire mechanic.

**How it works**

- Pick apps with Screen Time
- Choose a throttle level — Slow (256 kbps), Medium (1 Mbps), or Fast (5 Mbps)
- Tap Enable. iOS asks to install a VPN profile (on-device only — no server, no traffic leaves your device through us)
- Open a chosen app: your whole connection slows until you close it

**Honest about what iOS allows**

Throttle slows your *device's* connection while a chosen app is open, not just that app. iOS does not let App Store apps throttle individual apps in isolation. If you start streaming music and then open TikTok, the music will also stutter until you swipe TikTok away. We mention this here because we'd rather you know before downloading.

**24-hour lockout**

When you disable Throttle, you can't re-enable it for 24 hours. Commitment device on purpose.

**Private by default**

- No account
- No server (the VPN is on-device loopback)
- No analytics
- All data stays on your device
